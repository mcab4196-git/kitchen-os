1Kitchen OS — mobile redesign, full screen set + variations

What I read. The live app already ships everything in scope: dashboard, inventory list with search/filter/sort, add + edit, quick-use sheet, receipt scan + review, meals with AI suggestions, recipe detail with inventory reconciliation, weekly plan, shopping list, preferences. So this is a redesign of the mobile surface, not new features. Every route, action and state below maps to something that exists in src/app/(app)/.

Design system, unchanged. I kept your tokens verbatim — basil brand #2B584C, warm surface #FBFAF7, the four-role status scale (critical / soon / fresh / unknown), Fraunces display + Inter UI, 0.7rem control and 1rem card radii, 44px control height, the 480px app column. No new colors, no new fonts.

The three moves. (1) Triage before inventory — home leads with what needs eating today, not counts. (2) Grouped inventory — expiry buckets with sticky headers replace one flat list, so 60 items stay scannable one-handed. (3) A reachable primary action — a raised Add/Scan button above the tab bar, because the current header "Add" sits in the hardest-to-reach corner of a phone. The five labelled tabs stay exactly as they are.

Accessibility, held as constraints not polish. Status is always a word plus a shape, never color alone. Every hit target ≥44px. Labels are real <label>s above fields, not placeholders. Quantity changes and undo speak through a live region. Type scales with the OS and reflows at 200% zoom — no fixed-height text boxes. Focus rings use your existing brand outline.

40 min · Easy · serves 2

Steps

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the yoghurt with the cumin and a good pinch of salt.

  2. 2
